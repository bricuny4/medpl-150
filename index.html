Creative Statment

My name is Brianna, I'm a sophomore, and I go by she/her. I love to watch movies: my favorite genre is romcoms, but when I was younger I really enjoyed psychological-thrillers and still enjoy one once in a while these days. My favorite piece of media is Game of Thrones.

I aim to create media that is ultimately a reflection of myself and the things I care about. I think the production of art itself can be a way of making sense of both yourself and the world. What inspires my work is what is most relevant to me at the moment, whether its a movie that I just watched that I feel really strongly about, or something that happened in a relationship. I am quite drawn to art in the form of magazines, zines, visual novels/comic books, but I am also drawn to art in the form of film, photography, and writing.
